Crushing & grinding equipment for Africa

Africa is Boorant's main export market. We supply importers, distributors and retailers — including the suppliers who serve small and medium gold mine owners — with factory-direct jaw crushers, ball mills and complete crushing and grinding lines from Zhengzhou, China.

Priority markets

Zimbabwe

Strong demand from small and medium gold mines for compact jaw crushers and ball mills, and for the wear parts — jaw plates and mill liners — that keep them running. Diesel-powered crushers suit sites without reliable grid power.

Zambia

Gold and copper-belt mining demand for crushing and grinding equipment and spare parts, bought through local importers and mining suppliers.

Tanzania

Small-scale gold mining is a core buyer group for PE-series jaw crushers, 600×1200 ball mills and complete small lines configured to the ore and target capacity.

Francophone West & Central Africa

Guinea, Burkina Faso, Mali, Senegal and DR Congo: gold mining demand served in French. See our French page for product information in French, or contact us in French or English.

We also ship to other African markets — tell us your destination and we confirm the export documents and packing for that route in the quotation.

What African buyers usually ask us for

  • Small gold mining lines: a jaw crusher plus a ball mill sized to the ore, for example the PE200×300 (3–10 t/h, diesel or electric) with the 600×1200 ball mill (0.5–1.2 t/h, wet or dry).
  • Diesel power where the grid is weak: confirmed diesel options on the PE200×300 and PE250×400 jaw crushers.
  • Wear parts with the machine: jaw plates and other wear parts confirmed and included in the quotation, so the mine is not stopped waiting for spares.
  • One matching document set: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading draft, certificate of origin and CE documents when required, prepared consistently for clearance.
