Frequently asked questions

What does Boorant manufacture?

Boorant (Zhengzhou Boorant Mechanical Equipment Co., Ltd.) is a factory-direct manufacturer in Zhengzhou, China of jaw crushers in a full PE series, cone crushers, hammer crushers, impact crushers, ball mills, mobile crushing plants, vibrating screens and complete crushing and grinding lines. Its equipment is CE certified and its main export market is Africa.

Which jaw crusher is suitable for small gold mining?

For small gold mining, Boorant's PE200x300 jaw crusher is a compact primary crusher with a capacity of 3-10 t/h, powered by an electric motor or a diesel engine. The right model still depends on the ore hardness, maximum feed size, required output size and target capacity, which Boorant confirms before quoting.

What are the specifications of the PE250x400 jaw crusher?

The Boorant PE250x400 jaw crusher has a feed opening of 250x400 mm, a maximum feed size of 210 mm, a discharge opening of 20-70 mm, a capacity of 5-25 t/h and a 15 kW motor. It can be powered by an electric motor or a diesel engine.

Can a Boorant ball mill do both wet and dry grinding?

Yes. Boorant's 600x1200 ball mill supports both wet and dry grinding, with a capacity of 0.5-1.2 t/h, and is used for gold ore and other mineral processing.

Does Boorant supply complete crushing and grinding lines?

Yes. Boorant configures complete lines, for example a jaw crusher plus a vibrating screen plus a ball mill, matched to the buyer's material, feed size, output size and target capacity per hour.

How do I get a quotation from Boorant?

Send four inputs: material and hardness, feed size, required output size or fineness, and target capacity per hour. Boorant then recommends a model, confirms its real specification, and issues a quotation stating the Incoterm and port, currency, validity, payment terms, packing, lead time and warranty.
